Why Nutrics

Hitting your goals shouldn't mean cooking every meal. Here's what we're fixing, and who we're fixing it for.

Eating out shouldn't break your plan

Calorie apps hand you a grocery list. Delivery apps hand you a menu. Neither tells you what a meal actually does to your day — so the moment you eat out, you're guessing.

Built for your body, not the average one

Most nutrition tools are designed for an average person who doesn't exist. We built for the full range instead — every goal, every diet, every body. No one gets a shrunken version of someone else's plan.

Easy for restaurants to say yes to

None of this works if a kitchen can't do it. So we keep the ask simple: a few portion sizes for a dish they already make. No new recipes, no new equipment.
