The business

A marketplace, not a kitchen

Users build a health profile, browse meals from local restaurant partners, and check out for the meals they choose. Nutrics keeps a commission on every order — no subscription, no centralized kitchen, no inventory risk.

Personalized by design

Body metrics, preferences and goals are matched against local menus, so every plan is built for that person's targets — not a generic diet.

Local kitchens fulfil it

No owned kitchen or fleet. Restaurant partners prepare meals they already know how to make, in portion sizes we specify — variety and scale without our capex.

Revenue scales with orders

We earn a commission on gross order value, plus an optional split-delivery fee. Our incentive is repeat ordering, not just signups.

Traction & validation

Not starting from zero

  1. 2024

    Real customers, real feedback

    Ran a hands-on meal-prep and delivery business in the Brampton–Mississauga area — repeat customers, biweekly recipes, twice-weekly kitchen production. Their feedback (demand for goal-aligned meals, frustration with repetitive menus) is what drove the pivot to a platform model.

  2. Today

    MVP built and functional

    The mobile and web platform is built in-house — removing the largest execution risk for a software-led business. Public launch targeted for October 2026 in Toronto.

  3. Pre-launch

    Commitments before we open the doors

    500+ waitlist signups via gym partnerships and a landing page; 10+ signed vendor letters of intent converting to 30 onboarded restaurants by end of Year 1; a 50–100 person beta cohort from the 2024 customer base to generate real order-frequency and retention data pre-launch.
